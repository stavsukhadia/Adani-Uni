Historical Legacy & Mission

Rooted in Nation Building & Purpose

Established in 2022 as a State Private University in Gujarat, Adani University carries forward the educational legacy of the Adani Foundation, transforming young intellect into nation builders.

Vision of "Growth with Goodness"

Adani University was established under the Gujarat Private Universities Act, rooted firmly in the educational initiatives fostered by the Adani Foundation. Built upon the distinguished legacy of the Adani Institute of Infrastructure Management (AIIM), the university provides an environment where real-world industry immersion meets cutting-edge academic rigor.

Our pedagogical philosophy is inspired by Mr. Gautam Adani’s vision of nation building and infrastructure resilience, combined with Dr. Priti Adani’s dedication to transformative social empowerment through holistic education.

"Education must not simply be about imparting theoretical concepts; it must kindle the spirit of nation building, civic responsibility, and sustainable industrial growth."
— Dr. Priti Adani, Chairperson, Adani Foundation
